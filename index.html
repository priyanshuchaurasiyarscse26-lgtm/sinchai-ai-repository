How the model decides

    
    
      
  1. Window. Takes the last 5 minutes or the last 24 hours of readings and drops values outside sensor limits.
    2. 
      
  2. ETo (reference ET). FAO-56 Penman–Monteith from temperature, pressure, light and wind. Sunlight comes from the BH1750. Humidity is estimated from the night low (the BMP280 has no humidity sensor), and until the wind sensor is connected the Varanasi monthly average wind is used.
    3. 
      
  3. ETc = Kc × ETo. Kc follows the crop's growth stage, worked out from the sowing date.
    4. 
      
  4. Rain. Rain from the last 24 hours, made effective with the USDA-SCS rule, is subtracted. Rain below 1 mm is ignored.
    5. 
      
  5. Thresholds. Raining now → pause. Rain covers the need or soil is wet → skip. Heat, cold, wind and humidity add advice.
    6. 
      
  6. Litres. Net mm ÷ drip efficiency × 10,000 = litres per hectare per day, then the run time from your emitter layout.
